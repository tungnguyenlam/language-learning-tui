# Goal

deutsch-tui is a terminal application that makes learning German feel effortless and enjoyable.

A learner opens it, reviews a few cards, and walks away knowing a little more than before. The scheduling is invisible — it just works. The content is rich enough that a beginner can start from zero and an intermediate learner still finds it challenging. Decks can be browsed, searched, and imported without friction.

The AI features feel like a patient tutor sitting next to you: they explain, correct, and suggest without getting in the way of the core review loop.

The interface is calm, responsive, and polished. It adapts to the terminal it's running in. It never feels sluggish or broken. Keyboard users feel at home. Mouse users are not punished.

Everything lives locally. Your progress is yours. You can export it, back it up, or move it to another machine. Nothing phones home.

When someone asks "is there a good way to learn German from the terminal?", the answer is this app.
